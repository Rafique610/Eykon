# Phase 6 Step 12 — Live 30-Minute Meeting Run on Both Phones

## What

Roadmap v2 adds "a full 30-minute live meeting run". With consenting
participants (Phase 3.1 consent process), run a real 30-minute meeting with
the phone recording live (session mode, Phase 5.1), while the daily-life
pipeline is also active (as it would be in real use), on each phone:

- during the meeting: recording gaps, temperature, battery, the scheduler's
  handling of speech vs perception, optional live transcript if enabled;
- after the meeting: processing time to transcript, speakers, summary and
  action items (charging and not charging, per Phase 5.1 Step 11);
- quality: WER, DER, summary faithfulness (quick audit with the Phase 5.1
  Step 15 codes), action item precision / recall against the paper list,
  answers to 10 meeting questions asked afterwards;
- comparison with the replayed-meeting results from Phase 5.1 Step 18.

## Why

- Meetings are the committed new scenario; showing one recorded and processed
  live on a mid-range and a low-end phone is the strongest demo of it.
- Running alongside the daily-life pipeline tests the scheduler under the
  real combined load, not one feature at a time.

## How to implement

1. Use the Phase 3.1 meeting scenario sheets (new agenda) and consent forms.
2. One meeting per phone (two meetings in total), or both phones recording
   the same meeting side by side (open decision).
3. Telemetry and session outputs pulled; reports generated by the Phase 5.1
   evaluation scripts plus `field_report.py`.
4. Paper action items and a quick reference summary written right after.

## Open decisions

- **Same meeting on both phones or separate meetings.** Options: (a) both
  phones on the table recording the same meeting — direct comparison, same
  speech; (b) separate meetings — each phone gets a full real run, harder to
  compare; (c) (a) for one meeting, plus the Infinix alone for a second.
  Draft: (a).

## You test this

1. Lead the meeting normally; do not look at the phone.
2. Afterwards, read the summary and ask "what did I agree to do?"; check it
   against the paper list.

## How this number could be lying

- One or two live meetings are a demonstration plus measurement, not a
  statistically strong result; the Phase 5.1 results remain the main evidence
  for conversation quality.
- Participants who know the system may speak more clearly; note it.

- [ ] Processing times, battery, temperature during and after.
- [ ] Quality metrics computed and compared with Phase 5.1 replay results.

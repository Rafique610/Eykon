## What
Evaluate the final, human-readable answers generated by our local LLM (Gemma 4 E2B via LiteRT-LM) when it is fed the retrieved video memories. We will use a larger, frontier model (like Gemini or GPT-4o) as an automated "Judge" to score the local LLM's answers from 1 to 5.

## Why
While Experiments A1-A3 proved our system can successfully *find* the right memories (Retrieval/Hit@3), the panel may ask: *"But are the final conversational answers actually accurate and useful to the user?"* 
This step closes the loop. It proves that once the right memory is retrieved, the local mobile-friendly LLM is smart enough to generate a correct, hallucination-free response.

---

## Hypothesis
Given perfect retrieval context (Hit@K = 100%), the actual answer generated heavily depends on the depth and quality of the caption produced by the underlying VLM. We hypothesize that while all three VLMs (Moondream2, Gemma 4, SmolVLM) might retrieve the correct chunk, their final answer scores will vary wildly. We will use an LLM-as-a-Judge to measure this "True Answer Accuracy" for each VLM individually.

## Method
1. Take the existing 9 ground-truth QA queries from our test videos.
2. For each VLM candidate (Moondream2, Gemma 4, SmolVLM):
   - Search the database for the top-3 memory records generated by that VLM.
   - Construct the prompt: `Context: {retrieved_records} \n\n Question: {query}`
   - Generate the final answer using the local LLM.
3. Feed the `(Question, Ground Truth Context, Local LLM Answer)` into an external Judge LLM via API.
4. The Judge evaluates the answer on a scale of 1-5 based on:
   - **True Answer Accuracy:** Does it actually tell the user what they want to know (e.g., flow, location logic) rather than just stating "sunglasses exist"?
   - **Conciseness:** Is it direct and free of rambling?
   - **Hallucination:** Did it invent any details not present in the context?
5. Record the scores to identify the VLM that produces the highest-quality conversational context.

## Output Table

| VLM Source | Query | Generated Answer | Judge Score (1-5) | Judge Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SmolVLM | Where are my keys? | "Keys." | 2.0 | Too brief, lacks conversational flow or location context. |
| Moondream2 | Where are my keys? | "You left your keys on the kitchen counter." | 5.0 | Accurate, conversational, perfect flow. |
| Gemma 4 | Where are my keys? | "..." | — | — |

**Final Metric:** Average Judge Score across all 9 queries for each individual VLM.

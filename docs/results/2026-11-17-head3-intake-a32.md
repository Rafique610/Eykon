# Head 3 intake on the Samsung A32, next to the Pixel and the Infinix (17 Nov 2026)

Head 3 questions B1 (which stage takes the time, on every phone) and B2 (minutes of work per minute of video on the low-end phone).
**Why the A32 here** (rule: `docs/invariants/processes.md`, "Which phone a card runs on"): B2 asks about the slowest phone, and the A32
(Helio G80, 6 GB) is our slowest; the Pixel and the Infinix cannot answer it.

**Phone.** Samsung A32 (SM-A325F), Android 13, on the USB cable (so no battery figure), app in front.
**Build.** `feature/video-memory` at e4f2bd7, both APKs with `adb install -r`. This build has the faster frame split (d93c56b) and the EYKO-120
`latest` change, which the Pixel (a915c8d) and Infinix (5a192c7) runs did not have; it reads the video in place (no copy).
**How.** Harness `docs/phone-kit/run-head3.ps1`, S1, three runs per kit clip. Each run used a byte-different copy of the clip (a trailing
`free` atom of 8 to 48 bytes; same frames), added through the app's Video tab and the system file picker, driven over adb (the photos
permission prompt was declined each time, so no recording place was stored; that does not change the stages). Runs 2-6 by
`D:/eykon-cache/scratch/a32-runs.sh` (not in git).
**Files.** `head3/stage_times.csv`, `head3/run_summary.csv` (phone `SM-A325F`), `head3/raw/2026-11-17-SM_A325F-*`.

## Reading

- **B2:** the A32 needs **2.69-2.77 minutes of work per minute of video** on clip 1 and **4.46-4.53** on clip 2, with the app in front. That is
  1.5x the Infinix and 3.1x the Pixel. A 75 s clip is ready in 3.4 to 5.7 minutes.
- **B1 on the A32:** `small_objects` is the largest stage on clip 1 (68 of 203 s) and `latest` on clip 2 (147 of 339 s). The model stages
  (scene, small_objects, latest) are 74-83 % of the time. `small_objects` costs the A32 2.1x the Infinix, much more than any other stage.
- **Frame splitting:** 22.7-23.9 s on the A32 with the faster split; the decoder is most of it (`2026-11-17-split-frames-faster.md`).
- **Same output:** kept frames, objects and sightings match the other phones on the same clips (10 kept and 53 sightings on clip 1; 16 and 134 on clip 2).

## Limits

- Different builds across the three phones (see Build). The model stages did not change between them except `latest` (EYKO-120 made
  relinking cheaper, not the crop vectors), so the comparison holds for them; it does not for frame splitting.
- On the cable: no battery figure. Highest battery temperature 29.1-32.1 C.
- Two 75 s clips; no queue (S2), background or back-fill run on the A32 (background: EYKO-168).

## Incident during clean-up (17 Nov), and the repair

After the runs, the clean-up script deleted the six test folders **and the A32's four original video folders**: the saved folder list had two names
per line and the script read it as one per line. The database was restored byte-for-byte from the backup taken before the runs
(`D:\eykon-cache\backups\a32-head3-2026-11-17`, sha256 c8b306cf...). The three ready videos were repaired with the owner's go-ahead: each original
file put back in its folder (kit clips 1 and 2 from the laptop, the 12 Jun 2024 camera clip from the phone's own gallery), its job set back to the
first stage, and the app re-ran it. Afterwards kept frames, scene records, sightings (and which are latest) and texts were **identical** to the backup
for all four videos. Lost for good: the app's copy of the fourth video (8 Jul 2026, status failed, "no frame could be read"; it had no frames).
